THE LIBRARY YOU WANTED

Less setup theater.
More time playing.

GameDeck turns a scattered local collection into a coherent product: clear readiness checks, controller navigation, resilient downloads, honest diagnostics, and launch paths that explain themselves.

01

One-install runtime

Supported systems use a verified managed runtime and compatible engines without a separate emulator-install scavenger hunt.

02

Controller-first shelves

Browse systems, search, favorite, resume, and launch from the couch with adaptive keyboard, pointer, and gamepad guidance.

03

GameDeck Live

Capture the active game with audio and pair a browser or mobile receiver directly over your local network.

04

Remote Play Together

Invite P2–P4 through encrypted WebRTC. Video travels to friends; their native controller input returns to the host.

05

Arcade without mystery

MAME and FinalBurn Neo routes include real names, archive preflight, parent-file diagnostics, and scoped controller profiles.

Private by design
