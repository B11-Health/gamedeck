## Weekly operating rhythm

- **Monday:** cohort-status and gap review
- **Tuesday/Wednesday:** one focused tester call and one verified feature/proof post
- **Thursday:** setup, privacy, or reporting education
- **Friday/Saturday:** scheduled test windows and rule-compatible outreach
- **Sunday:** evidence review, sanitization, approval, and next-week plan

## Calendar rollback

If a claim is rejected, a platform blocks the post, consent is unclear, or a report contains sensitive data, stop the scheduled item. Use the official open-graph image and a plain recruitment update as the safe fallback, or publish nothing. Never replace a blocked post with repeated reposts, account creation, paid promotion, or broader claims.
