# GameDeck Growth Launch Kit

## Positioning

**One-line:** GameDeck is a cinematic, controller-first, local-first launcher for the games you legally own.

**Promise:** Bring your own library and supported emulators; GameDeck turns them into one living-room-ready collection without accounts or cloud lock-in.

**Trust line:** GameDeck includes no ROMs, BIOS files, encryption keys, or commercial game artwork.

## Launch post — default

I built GameDeck because a legally owned game collection should feel like a collection, not a folder tree.

It is an open-source, controller-first desktop launcher for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It scans local files, checks emulator readiness, routes titles to supported emulators, caches artwork, and gives the whole library a cinematic interface.

Highlights:
- local-first and account-free
- controller, keyboard, and mouse navigation
- RetroArch plus standalone emulator routing
- visible BIOS and setup diagnostics
- favorites, recent plays, search, and Surprise Me
- MIT licensed

GameDeck does not include games, firmware, keys, or commercial artwork. Bring only content you are legally entitled to use.

Download: https://b11-health.github.io/gamedeck/

Trailer: https://youtu.be/0nCHy9WsEpQ

## Ethical launch checklist

1. Post only from authentic creator/project accounts.
2. Read and follow each community's self-promotion rules.
3. Do not buy votes, reviews, followers, stars, or downloads.
4. Do not mass-DM users or scrape contact lists.
5. Disclose creator affiliation.
6. Answer technical criticism directly and log reproducible issues.
7. Track weekly: landing visits, release downloads, stars, issues opened, first-run success, and seven-day retention once privacy-preserving telemetry exists.
