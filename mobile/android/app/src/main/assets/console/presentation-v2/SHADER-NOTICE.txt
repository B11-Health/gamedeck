GameDeck Ambient Blur Fill

The files under shaders/ and blurs/, plus blur_fill.glslp, are derived from
libretro/glsl-shaders "Blur Fill" by fishku, copyright 2023-2025, dedicated
to the public domain under CC0. Source:
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders/tree/master/borders

GameDeck packages the shader locally so Android gameplay has a deterministic,
o-network ambient fill presentation.
