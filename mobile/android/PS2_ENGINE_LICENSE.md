# Embedded PlayStation 2 engine licensing

The GameDeck Android APK includes a PCSX2-derived ARM64 engine from ARMSX2 2.6.6.4, commit `dd5b6616eb315260b235fee36083e1ba45fc36a0`.

ARMSX2 and PCSX2-derived portions are licensed under GNU GPL version 3. The complete GPL text and source-location notice are packaged in the APK under `assets/licenses/` and retained in the `ps2engine` module.

The GameDeck integration source, including the dependency orchestrator and GameDeck-owned Android activity, is distributed with the GameDeck source tree. No proprietary PlayStation 2 firmware or commercial game content is bundled.
