## Result

**PASS — no known release-blocking defects after the completed Windows, packaged-runtime, desktop UI, public-site, mobile-receiver, multiplayer, security, dependency, link, and repository-integrity checks described below.**

This report records evidence, not a claim that software can never contain another defect. Platform limitations are listed explicitly at the end.
