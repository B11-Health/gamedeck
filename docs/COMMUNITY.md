## Multiplayer coordination

Use Discussion #8 to post the game, play style, time zone, start window, and whether you can host. Do not place a live `GDREMOTE2`, `GDREMOTEANSWER2`, or `GDPLAY1` code in a public discussion. Invitation codes are short-lived private connection material; exchange the fresh code through a private channel you trust after choosing a player.

For synchronized netplay, both players need matching local game and core IDs. For Remote Play Together, only the host needs the game. GameDeck does not transfer ROMs, firmware, saves, or account credentials.

## Community standards

- Share only games and firmware you legally own. Do not post ROM, BIOS, key, or copyrighted-asset downloads.
- Never request passwords, access tokens, private keys, or remote-desktop access.
- Keep support reports specific: operating system, GameDeck version, system/core, game title, controller, exact steps, and Status Center output.
- Remove personal paths, invitation payloads, session identifiers, and secrets before posting logs.
- Disclose when you maintain or promote GameDeck. Do not manufacture stars, comments, testimonials, or engagement.
- Be constructive and welcoming. Harassment, impersonation, spam, and unsafe downloads are not accepted.
