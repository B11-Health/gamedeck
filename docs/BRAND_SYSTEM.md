# GameDeck Brand System

GameDeck uses a premium cinematic identity built around midnight navy, electric cyan, and controlled violet light. The emblem remains the primary recognition asset; the wordmark and tagline support it without competing with it.

## Core lockup

- Primary name: **GameDeck**
- Display wordmark: **GAMEDECK**
- Tagline: **One library. Every era.**
- Voice: confident, cinematic, precise, open-source, never childish

## Asset kit

All production exports live in `assets/branding/brand-kit/`.

- Avatars: 256, 400, 512, and 1024 px
- Transparent wordmarks: dark-background and light-background variants
- Social headers: GitHub, Open Graph, X, LinkedIn, and YouTube; the former Discord banner remains a legacy archive and must not be deployed
- App splash art: 1920×1080
- Monochrome marks: white and dark
- Machine-readable manifest: `manifest.json`

## Usage rules

1. Keep clear space around the emblem equal to at least 20% of its width.
2. Never place the emblem over busy artwork without a dark field or glow separation.
3. Do not recolor the emblem with platform-specific colors.
4. Use cyan for actions and focus; use violet sparingly for depth and atmosphere.
5. Avoid neon green, generic gamer red, console logos, recognizable game art, and trademarked controller silhouettes.
6. Preserve the safe central area on YouTube and responsive banner crops.
