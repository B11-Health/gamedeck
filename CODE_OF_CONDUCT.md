# Community Code of Conduct

GameDeck welcomes players, preservation-minded developers, emulator maintainers, designers, and first-time contributors.

Be constructive, specific, patient, and respectful. Debate ideas without attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, demean, dox, spam, or post sexual, hateful, or discriminatory content. Respect privacy, licenses, platform rules, and applicable law.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub Security Advisories. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
